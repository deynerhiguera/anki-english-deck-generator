Card Philosophy

Mission

This project does not create vocabulary cards.

It creates opportunities to think, understand and communicate in English.

The purpose is to help me become fluent enough to naturally express my ideas, understand other people, participate in technical conversations, work in English and eventually think in English without translating from Spanish.

Every generated card should contribute to real communication rather than memorization.

Vocabulary is only a tool.

Communication is the real objective.

⸻

Core Principles

Every generated card should feel as if I had written it myself.

The application should never behave like a dictionary.

It should behave like an English coach that understands my goals and generates vocabulary I will actually use.

The objective is to build intuition, not simply store definitions.

Every decision should optimize long-term language acquisition.

⸻

⸻

Priorities for Example Generation

Whenever possible, examples should follow this priority order:

1. Situations I will probably experience in my future.
2. Software engineering and professional work.
3. University and learning.
4. Learning English.
5. Daily life.
6. Personal growth and mindset.
7. Productivity and discipline.
8. Gym.
9. Basketball.

Do not force these topics.

Choose the one that produces the most natural sentence.

⸻

Meaning Generation

Always generate meanings in English.

Never translate into Spanish.

Do not copy dictionary definitions.

Write the meaning the way a good English teacher would explain it.

The meaning should explain how the word is actually used.

Prefer clarity over precision.

If multiple meanings exist, choose the one that is most useful for communication.

⸻

Example Generation

This is the most important field of every card.

Examples should sound like real thoughts.

The sentence should feel like something I could genuinely say tomorrow.

Avoid textbook English.

Avoid artificial examples.

Avoid unrealistic situations.

Avoid examples that exist only to demonstrate grammar.

Generate language that sounds alive.

The examples should help me express ideas I actually care about.

Whenever possible, generate examples related to my projects, work, studies, ambitions, habits, routines or future goals.

⸻

Language Growth

Do not intentionally simplify every sentence.

Natural English contains words that I may not know yet.

That is desirable.

If an example introduces interesting vocabulary that fits naturally, keep it.

Those unknown words can become future cards.

The objective is to expose me to authentic English, not artificially simplified English.

⸻

Communication Style

The generated English should resemble the language used by:

* experienced software engineers
* coworkers
* technical meetings
* YouTube creators
* podcasts
* documentation
* everyday conversations
* motivated people talking about learning and improving

The English should feel modern and useful.

⸻

Mindset

Whenever natural, examples may include ideas related to:

* learning
* improving
* consistency
* discipline
* persistence
* hard work
* building projects
* solving problems
* taking responsibility
* becoming better every day
* achieving long-term goals

These ideas reflect the type of language I want to internalize.

However, never force motivational sentences when they do not fit the target word.

Naturalness always comes first.

⸻

Active Recall

The cloze sentence should hide only the target word or expression.

The remaining sentence should provide enough context to retrieve the answer naturally.

The sentence should encourage recall, not guessing.

⸻

Expressions and Phrasal Verbs

Treat expressions as complete vocabulary units.

Never separate them.

Examples include:

* stick to
* get rid of
* come up with
* look forward to

Teach how people actually use them.

⸻

IPA

Always generate American IPA.

Use the pronunciation most commonly heard in modern American English.

⸻

Audio

Generate natural pronunciation.

One audio for the vocabulary item.

One audio for the complete example.

The audio should sound clear and natural.

⸻

Consistency

Every generated card should look indistinguishable from a card I would have created manually.

Consistency is more important than creativity.

The user should never notice whether the card was created by AI or by hand.

⸻

Quality Checklist

Before accepting a card, verify:

* Does the meaning explain actual usage instead of defining the word?
* Does the example sound like something I could genuinely say?
* Would this sentence help me communicate better?
* Does the sentence feel natural?
* Is this vocabulary useful for the kind of life I want to live?
* Does the card encourage long-term acquisition rather than memorization?
* Does the card match my learning philosophy?

If any answer is “No”, regenerate the card.

⸻

Final Principle

Do not ask:

“What is the correct sentence?”

Ask:

“What sentence would help this person become a more confident English speaker?”

Every generated card should move me one step closer to living part of my life in English.
